Abstract

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks. We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely.

Sticky note
Summarize the key contribution of the Transformer architecture paper.

The Transformer's key contribution is replacing recurrence and convolution entirely with self-attention. The scaled dot-product attention mechanism lets every position attend to all others in parallel, enabling significantly faster training on GPUs while achieving state-of-the-art results on translation tasks.
