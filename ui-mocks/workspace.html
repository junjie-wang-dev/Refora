Scaling laws notes

Jul 10, 2024

Loss scales as a power law with compute. The exponent depends on dataset size, model capacity, and the batch size used during training.

Key takeaways

  • Doubling compute yields predictable loss reductions.
  • Optimal allocation favors larger models over more data, up to a point.
